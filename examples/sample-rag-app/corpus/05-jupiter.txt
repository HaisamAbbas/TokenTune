Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System, with a mass more than twice
that of all other planets combined. It is a gas giant composed mainly of
hydrogen and helium. Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a giant storm that has raged
for at least 350 years and is large enough to swallow Earth several times over.
Jupiter has at least 95 known moons, the four largest of which -- Io, Europa,
Ganymede, and Callisto -- are called the Galilean moons.
