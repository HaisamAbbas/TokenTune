Neptune is the eighth and farthest known planet from the Sun and an ice giant
like Uranus. It has the strongest sustained winds of any planet in the Solar
System, reaching speeds of over 2,000 kilometers per hour. Neptune was the
first planet located through mathematical prediction rather than direct
observation, after astronomers noticed irregularities in Uranus's orbit. Its
largest moon, Triton, orbits backwards relative to Neptune's rotation.
