Mars, often called the Red Planet because of iron oxide on its surface, is the
fourth planet from the Sun. It has the largest volcano in the Solar System,
Olympus Mons, which stands about 22 kilometers high, and a canyon system called
Valles Marineris that stretches over 4,000 kilometers. Mars has two small
moons, Phobos and Deimos, and a thin atmosphere made mostly of carbon dioxide.
Several rovers, including Curiosity and Perseverance, have explored its surface.
