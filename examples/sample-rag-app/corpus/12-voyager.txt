The Voyager program launched two robotic probes, Voyager 1 and Voyager 2, in
1977 to study the outer planets. Voyager 2 remains the only spacecraft to have
visited Uranus and Neptune. Both probes have since left the heliosphere and
entered interstellar space, making them the most distant human-made objects
from Earth. Each carries a Golden Record with sounds and images meant to
represent life and culture on Earth, intended for any intelligent life that
might find it.
