The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite and the fifth-largest moon in the
Solar System. It formed roughly 4.5 billion years ago, likely from debris left
after a Mars-sized body collided with the early Earth. The Moon is
tidally locked to Earth, so the same side always faces us. Apollo 11 landed
the first humans on the Moon on July 20, 1969, with Neil Armstrong becoming the
first person to walk on its surface.
